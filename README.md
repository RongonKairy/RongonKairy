## About

I am a technology-focused builder interested in **software engineering, artificial intelligence, full-stack development, developer tools, and product engineering**.

My work centers on turning ideas into useful digital products through clean architecture, practical automation, modern web technologies, and AI-powered workflows. I enjoy exploring emerging technologies, evaluating developer tools, experimenting with open-source software, and building solutions that prioritize usability, performance, and maintainability.

I approach engineering with a **product mindset**: understand the problem, design the simplest reliable solution, build with scalable foundations, measure the result, and continuously improve.

- **Software Engineering** — clean architecture, maintainable systems, APIs, automation, and scalable application design
- **AI / ML** — AI applications, LLM workflows, intelligent automation, model-powered products, and AI tooling
- **Full Stack Development** — modern frontend interfaces, backend services, databases, APIs, and deployment
- **Product Engineering** — problem discovery, rapid prototyping, iteration, usability, and production-oriented development
- **Open Source** — exploring open-source ecosystems, developer utilities, AI projects, and practical tooling
- **Technical Content** — researching and sharing useful AI, software, and developer technologies
